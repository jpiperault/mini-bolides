Mini Bolides privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Publisher: Jupi Studio · Contact: contact@jupistudio.com

In short: Mini Bolides collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server. The game works fully offline.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, cars, upgrades, paints, stars, cups, trophies, best times and ghost laps, daily gift, challenge and missions) and your settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no name or email requested.
  • No internet connection: the version of the app published on Google Play does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything online.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDK, no advertising ID.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No in-app purchases in the current version: coins are only won on the track.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional, only if you accept): one local reminder a day when your daily gift and new missions are ready. It is scheduled on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the game settings or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup: only used to keep that reminder scheduled after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback during races, which you can turn off in the game settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example purchases or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
